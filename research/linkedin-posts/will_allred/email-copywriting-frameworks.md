# Post: The 30-Word Rule in B2B Cold Email

**Expert:** Will Allred (Co-Founder of Lavender)  
**Platform:** LinkedIn Organic Strategy  

## Content & Strategy Breakdown

### The Framework
"Most cold emails fail because they read like essays. Executives read emails on their phones between meetings. If they have to scroll, they delete."

### Key Metrics Shared by Lavender Data
* Emails under 30 words see an average response rate increase of 40% compared to longer alternatives.
* Keep the subject line short: 1 to 3 words max. Long subject lines look like newsletters and get ignored.
* Focus on a clear, low-friction Call to Action (CTA). Instead of asking for a 30-minute meeting, ask a simple question like: *"Are you open to exploring this next week?"*

### Tactical Application for B2B SaaS
1. Write the draft.
2. Cut 50% of the words.
3. Optimize the first line to ensure it displays perfectly in the mobile notification preview without looking generic (avoid *"I hope this email finds you well"*).
